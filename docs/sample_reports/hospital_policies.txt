MediAgent General Hospital — Patient Policies

Visiting Hours
General ward visiting hours are 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily. A maximum of two visitors per patient is allowed at a time. ICU visiting hours are limited to 15 minutes, twice a day (11:00 AM and 6:00 PM), and only for immediate family members. Maternity ward visiting hours follow the general ward schedule, with additional restrictions during the first 24 hours after delivery. The emergency ward has no fixed visiting hours; visitors should check with the duty nurse, as access may be restricted depending on the patient's condition.

Admission Process
Patients being admitted should bring a valid photo ID, any referral letters from their doctor, and a list of current medications. Admission can be arranged through the Emergency Department for urgent cases or through the outpatient department for scheduled procedures. A refundable admission deposit is required at the time of admission for non-emergency cases; the amount depends on the department and expected length of stay.

Discharge Process
Discharge typically takes place between 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM to allow time for bed turnover. Patients will receive a discharge summary, a list of prescribed medications, and follow-up appointment details before leaving. Discharge against medical advice requires a signed waiver form.

Billing and Insurance
The hospital accepts most major insurance providers; patients should confirm coverage with the billing desk before admission where possible. For cashless treatment, pre-authorization from the insurance provider is required at least 48 hours before a planned procedure, or as soon as possible in an emergency. Payment plans are available for patients facing financial hardship — ask the billing office for details.

Patient Rights
Patients have the right to receive clear information about their diagnosis, treatment options, and any associated risks before consenting to treatment. Patients may request a second opinion at any time. All patient records are kept confidential and are only shared with other providers with the patient's consent, except where required by law.

Emergency Department Priority
The Emergency Department uses a triage system to prioritize patients based on the severity of their condition, not on arrival time. Life-threatening conditions such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or loss of consciousness are treated immediately. Patients with less urgent conditions may experience longer wait times during busy periods.
