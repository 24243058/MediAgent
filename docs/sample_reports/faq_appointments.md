# Appointment & Scheduling FAQ

## How far in advance can I book an appointment?
Routine specialist appointments can generally be booked up to 60 days in advance. Same-day appointments are available for urgent (non-emergency) concerns, subject to doctor availability.

## What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment time whenever possible. Repeated no-shows without notice may require a deposit for future bookings.

## How long is a typical appointment?
Standard consultations are scheduled for 15–20 minutes. Complex cases, first-time consultations, or procedures may require a longer slot — let the front desk know when booking if you expect this.

## Can I request a specific doctor?
Yes, if a specific doctor is available in the relevant department. If your requested doctor is fully booked, the system will offer the next available doctor in the same specialty.

## What should I bring to my appointment?
Bring a valid photo ID, your insurance details if applicable, a list of current medications, and any relevant previous test results or referral letters.

## Are walk-in appointments available?
The General Medicine and Pediatrics departments accept a limited number of walk-in patients each day, seen on a first-come, first-served basis after scheduled appointments. Specialist departments such as Cardiology, Dermatology, and Orthopedics require a booked appointment.
